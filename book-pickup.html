A LITTLE PREP GOES A LONG WAY.

Make pickup day easy.

Three small details can help explain what you want for each garment. Open a card for a quick tip.

01Choose your pieces+

Group the garments you want to alter or repair. Note the service and quantity for each piece so your request is easy to review.

02Share your ideal fit+

A reference garment or a few fit notes can explain the shape and length you prefer. You can request measurement assistance if you are unsure.

03Point out the details+

Mention loose fastenings, damaged seams or areas that feel too tight or too long. Put each garment’s instructions in its own request entry.
