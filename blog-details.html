How to know when clothes need alterations

Fit guide

Start with the way it feels

Try the garment with the shoes and layers you normally wear. Move, sit and reach as you would during the day. Make a note of the areas that pull, bunch or feel uncomfortable.

Describe the result you want

Clear requirements are more useful than simply asking for a better fit. Explain whether you want more room, less length or a different silhouette. A garment you already enjoy wearing can provide a helpful reference.

Consider the garment construction

Seams, lining, fastenings and fabric influence what can be altered. Some garments have limited seam allowance. Ask for a review before assuming a particular change is possible.

Prepare the garment for collection

Empty pockets and attach separate notes to each piece. Follow the care label and clean the garment where appropriate. Keep references and measurement notes together.

Review before proceeding

Confirm the planned work, price estimate and collection arrangements. When your garment returns, check the fit and care for it according to the original label and any specific instructions.
